THE CREED

An order built on three certainties.

"We do not answer to crowns. We answer to what is owed, and to no one else."
I

Move where none are watching.The Order trains the body to vanish before it trains the hand to strike. Silence is the first weapon; every blade after it is a formality.

II

Strike once, and let it be enough.An Ordo blade is never drawn twice for the same purpose. What we make is built to end a matter cleanly, not to be relied upon.

III

Leave the world as you found it — untold.No banner, no signature, no witness. The mark of our craft is that it was never noticed at all.

THE ARMORY

Instruments for those who work in the space between heartbeats.

The Order does not recruit.
It only recognizes.
